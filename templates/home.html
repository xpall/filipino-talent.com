How it works

{% include "components/how_it_works.html" %}
  1. Pick a name and sign in. A subdomain is just the part before the dot — like juan-dela-cruz. Sign in with Google, choose the name you want, and tell us your portfolio URL and where it is hosted.
  2. It goes live. Your address is published immediately. Google confirms it is really you, so there is no confirmation email to send. A maintainer reviews published subdomains afterward.
  3. Connect it at your host. Tell GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, Vercel, or Netlify to answer for the subdomain. Our one-page guide walks you through it.
  4. Keep it, or pass it on. It is yours for as long as you need it. Once you can afford your own domain, release it from your account for the next person.
