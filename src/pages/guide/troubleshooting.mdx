# Troubleshooting

## How long until my subdomain is live?

Usually within 15 minutes. It publishes automatically. To help us confirm it is
yours, email `verify@filipino-talent.com` from the address shown on your
portfolio (no code needed). If we do not hear from you within a day, we remove
the subdomain. A maintainer also reviews published subdomains and may remove any
that break the rules.

## Why was my subdomain removed?

We review subdomains after they publish and remove ones that break the
[Terms](/terms), such as impersonation, phishing, or illegal content.

## My subdomain shows 522, "404", or a host error page

You have not connected the subdomain at your host yet. DNS alone is not enough —
the host also needs to know it should answer for your subdomain.

- Cloudflare Pages shows **522 Connection timed out** until you add the domain
  under **Custom domains**.
- Other hosts show a 404 or their own error page.

Follow the [Connect your subdomain](/guide/connect) steps:

- GitHub Pages → Settings → Pages → Custom domain
- Cloudflare Pages → Custom domains
- Netlify → Domain management
- Vercel → Settings → Domains

## It says the site is "not secure" or shows an HTTPS warning

SSL certificates are issued automatically by your host after DNS is detected,
but the first one can take up to an hour. Wait, then reload. On GitHub Pages,
also tick **Enforce HTTPS** once it is available.

## The subdomain does not load at all

- DNS can take a few minutes to spread. Try again after 10 minutes.
- Make sure your portfolio URL itself works. If the host site is down, the
  subdomain will be down too.
- Clear your browser cache or try a private window.

## I changed my host and my subdomain stopped working

Your CNAME target changed, so we need to update the record. Email
`verify@filipino-talent.com` with your subdomain and the new target.

## Can I use my Canva site?

No. Free Canva cannot use a custom domain. See
[Using Canva](/guide/canva) for free alternatives.

## Can I get email at my subdomain?

No. This service only creates web (DNS) records. It does not provide mailboxes.

## I want a different subdomain, or to remove mine

Email `verify@filipino-talent.com` with the current and desired name, or
`remove@filipino-talent.com` to remove your subdomain. Note that names that are
released can be taken by someone else later.

## My request was rejected

Common reasons: the subdomain is reserved, the target host is not supported, the
name appears to impersonate someone, or we could not confirm the portfolio is
yours. You can fix the issue and [submit again](/submit).
