# Using Canva (and other builders)

A lot of people build their portfolio in Canva. There is one important catch:

> **Free Canva websites cannot use a custom domain.** Connecting your own domain
> is a Canva Pro feature, and Canva does not accept a plain external CNAME the
> way GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, Netlify or Vercel do.

We cannot add a DNS record that points at a Canva free site, because there is no
target address on Canva's side that will answer to your subdomain. It would
produce a broken page.

## Your options

### Option A: Rebuild a quick page for free (recommended)

If you already have the design in Canva, you can recreate a simple one-page
portfolio in about 30 minutes:

- **Carrd:** free plan supports a custom domain. Recreate your sections, then
  follow the same [submit](/submit) flow with target `yourname.carrd.co`.
- **GitHub Pages:** free. See the [GitHub Pages guide](/guide/github-pages).
  You can export your Canva design as images and place them on the page.

### Option B: Keep the Canva share link

Your published Canva site already has a link like
`https://yourname.my.canva.site`. You can share that directly. It will not use
`filipino-talent.com`, but it works today.

### Option C: Canva Pro

If you upgrade to Canva Pro, Canva's custom-domain setup uses its own DNS
records. Contact us at `verify@filipino-talent.com` before submitting and we will
check whether the required target can be supported.

## Other builders

- **Webflow, Notion, Wix, Squarespace, WordPress.com, Medium:** custom domains
  are paid features and generally need their own verification flow, so they are
  not on our allow-list.
- If your host gives you a target like `something.example.com` and you are not
  sure it works, email us and we will take a look.
